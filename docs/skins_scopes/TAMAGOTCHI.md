Detailed Design Description of the Tamagotchi Aesthetic
This visual direction is rooted in late-1990s handheld virtual pet hardware, combining a playful, plastic physical shell with a low-tech monochrome LCD screen. The aesthetic mixes bright "Memphis Group"-inspired pop graphics with retro digital displays.

1. Outer Shell Chassis and Plastic Texture

Smooth Oval Geometry: The main enclosure is a organic, egg-shaped curved shell. The material is a smooth, semi-gloss molded plastic with soft highlight reflections, giving it a tactile, toy-like appearance.

Vibrant Pop Art Graphics: The plastic body is covered in loud 90s street-style illustrations—jagged lightning bolts, starburst explosions, and geometric comic accents.

Jagged Screen Bezel: The central display window is framed by a sharp, multi-pointed starburst or jagged polygon border that creates a bold color transition between the outer shell and the inner screen area.

2. LCD Display Screen Aesthetic

Recessed Square Window: The active display area is a small, inset square screen with a subtle drop shadow along its inner edges, giving the glass panel depth inside the plastic shell.

Low-Contrast Olive/Grey LCD Matrix: The screen background simulates an unlit, passive-matrix LCD panel with a pale olive-yellow tint (#C5CE73 to #E1E897).

Dynamic Background Pattern: Behind the sprites, the LCD panel features a low-contrast, wavy/swirling line pattern printed directly onto the background layer in a slightly darker olive shade.

Fixed Icon Margins: The top and bottom rows of the screen feature a distinct light-grey band reserved for static UI status icons (feed, light, play, medicine, bathroom, status, discipline, attention).

3. Sprite Graphics and Pixelation

Coarse Monochrome Pixel Art: Central characters and icons are rendered in pure black (#000000) against the olive display, with zero grayscale shades or anti-aliasing.

Extremely Low Resolution Grid: Sprites use a minimalist grid (roughly 16x16 to 32x32 pixels), relying on simple, recognizable silhouettes with solid black block fills and empty spaces.

Static UI Icons: The status bar icons along the top and bottom borders are drawn with thin, hand-doodled pixel outlines, resembling simple iconographic line art.

4. Color Palette and Lighting

Vivid Toy Shell Accents: High-saturation, contrasting plastic tones define the outer frame:

Sky Blue: Primary cyan/sky-blue (#2DBAEB) for the main body.

Magenta/Hot Pink: Intense magenta (#D61380) for the screen bezel and graphic accents.

Bright Yellow: Electric yellow (#FCE013) for lightning graphics and physical hardware accents.

Monochrome Screen Contrast: Inside the display window, color is entirely restricted to the high-contrast contrast between black pixels, olive-tinted screen glass, and muted grey icon strips.

Soft Ambient Lighting: Unlike neon CRT effects, lighting here is ambient and reflective, mimicking a plastic toy under room light with soft highlights along rounded plastic curves.
